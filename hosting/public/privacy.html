SnapLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

SnapLoop helps people participating in shared Events find photos of themselves taken on their own or other participating users' devices. This Privacy Policy explains the personal information SnapLoop processes, why it is processed, how it may be shared, how long it is retained, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

  • Phone number: used to create and authenticate your SnapLoop account through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile information: such as your optional display name and account settings.
  • Event information: including Event name, dates, category, membership, invitations, and Event settings.
  • Photos and photo-library information: SnapLoop accesses photos only after you grant iOS Photos permission. Event scanning is limited to photos eligible for the selected Event date range.
  • Matched photo previews: optimized previews of photos identified as relevant to participating users may be uploaded so authorized users can view matched photos within the Event.
  • Face Setup information: if you expressly consent to Face Match, SnapLoop creates numerical face-template metadata (face embeddings and related templates) from your Face Setup selfie. The selfie/reference images themselves remain on your iPhone and are not uploaded to SnapLoop.
  • Consent records: SnapLoop records the applicable Face Match consent-policy version and timestamps showing when consent was given or withdrawn. These records do not contain your selfie or face template.
  • Technical information: limited device, diagnostic, crash, security, notification, and service information may be processed by Apple, Firebase, or other infrastructure providers used to operate SnapLoop.

2. How We Use Information

We process information only for purposes reasonably connected to operating SnapLoop, including to:

  • authenticate users;
  • create, join, administer, and secure Events;
  • perform Face Match when the user has provided active consent;
  • identify and deliver matched photos to authorized Event participants;
  • support invitations, notifications, saving, sharing, and Event functionality;
  • maintain security, reliability, abuse prevention, and app performance; and
  • investigate crashes, errors, fraud, misuse, or technical problems.

SnapLoop does not use face-template metadata for advertising, account authentication, surveillance, stranger identification, or to infer sensitive characteristics such as race, religion, health status, sexual orientation, or political views.

3. Face Match and Express Consent

Face Match is optional. Before Face Setup creates or stores face-template metadata, SnapLoop presents a separate Face Match consent notice and requires an affirmative I Agree & Continue action.

The consent notice explains the type of biometric information involved, the purpose of processing, where the data is stored, how it is used in Events, retention, and the consequences of refusing or withdrawing consent.

Your Face Setup selfie/reference images remain on your iPhone. Numerical face-template metadata is stored with your SnapLoop account in Firebase. While you participate in an active Event, your face-template metadata may be made available only to authenticated participating Event members' devices so that matching can run on-device against eligible Event-date photos.

Age: Face Match is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. Users who are not eligible to provide valid consent should not activate Face Match.

4. Withdrawing Face Match Consent

You can review or withdraw Face Match consent at any time from Privacy & Data in SnapLoop. Withdrawal is designed to be available without deleting your SnapLoop account.

When you confirm withdrawal, SnapLoop stops future Face Match processing and deletes your Face Setup, account-level face-template metadata, and related face-matching data from active Event metadata. Your local Face Setup reference image is also removed from the device. You will no longer receive automatically matched photos until you complete Face Setup and give consent again.

A limited consent record, including when consent was given or withdrawn and the applicable policy version, may be retained where reasonably necessary to demonstrate consent history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain security and audit records.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

SnapLoop does not sell personal information. Information is disclosed only as needed to provide the service, comply with law, protect users or SnapLoop, or with your direction or consent.

Within an Event, matched photo information and previews may be made available to authorized Event participants as required for the Event's photo-sharing functionality. Face-template metadata is not intended to be displayed to users; it is used by participating devices for Face Match processing.

SnapLoop uses service providers including Google Firebase for authentication, databases, cloud storage, notifications, and backend services, and Apple platform services for iOS functionality. These providers process information on SnapLoop's behalf subject to their applicable terms, security measures, and data-protection obligations.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Face Setup: account-level face-template metadata is retained while Face Match remains configured, unless you delete Face Setup, withdraw consent, or delete your account.
  • Event data: Event-related cloud data, including matched photo previews, is deleted within 15 days after an Event ends. If an Event is manually deleted, its Event-related cloud data is deleted within 15 days of deletion.
  • Account deletion: the in-app Delete Account control deletes the SnapLoop account and associated account data, subject to limited information that may need to be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or backup rotation.
  • Consent records: a limited record of consent or withdrawal may be retained as described above.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information. SnapLoop provides controls that allow you to:

  • deny, limit, or revoke Camera and Photos permissions through iOS Settings;
  • choose not to activate Face Match;
  • review or withdraw Face Match consent;
  • delete Face Setup without deleting your account;
  • leave Events where supported;
  • delete your SnapLoop account in the app; and
  • contact us regarding access, correction, deletion, consent, or privacy questions.

To exercise a privacy right or ask a privacy question, contact g.chhiber3@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SnapLoop's Face Match feature is intended only for users age 18 or older. SnapLoop does not knowingly intend to process children's biometric information without any consent or authorization required by applicable law. If you believe a child has activated Face Match improperly, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate deletion or restriction steps.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Safeguards are selected with regard to the sensitivity of the information. No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Processing

SnapLoop uses cloud and platform service providers whose systems may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, data is handled subject to the contractual, technical, organizational, and legal safeguards available for those services and jurisdictions.

11. Changes to This Policy or Face Match Purpose

We may update this Privacy Policy as SnapLoop evolves. The date at the top identifies the current version. If SnapLoop materially changes the purpose for which face-template metadata is collected, used, or disclosed, SnapLoop will require review of the updated Face Match notice and new consent where required before using the information for that materially changed purpose.
