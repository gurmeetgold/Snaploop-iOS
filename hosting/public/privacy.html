SnapLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

SnapLoop is designed to help people participating in shared events find photos of themselves taken on their own or other participants' devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information SnapLoop processes, why it is processed, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use SnapLoop, we may process:

  • Phone number: used to create and authenticate your SnapLoop account through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile information: such as your display name and account-related settings.
  • Event information: including Event name, dates, category, membership, invitations, and related Event settings.
  • Photos: SnapLoop accesses photos only after you grant Photos permission. Event scanning is limited to photos eligible for the Event date range.
  • Matched photo previews: optimized previews of photos identified as relevant to Event participants may be uploaded so they can be displayed and shared within the Event.
  • Face Setup information: SnapLoop creates facial templates or embeddings from Face Setup images for the purpose of identifying photos of participating users. Face matching is performed on-device. Face-related templates may also be stored or synchronized through Firebase as necessary to support Event matching and participant functionality.
  • Technical information: limited device, diagnostic, crash, security, and service information may be processed by Apple, Firebase, or other infrastructure providers used to operate SnapLoop.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • authenticate users;
  • create, join, and manage Events;
  • identify photos that may contain participating users;
  • display matched photos to authorized Event participants;
  • support invitations, notifications, and Event functionality;
  • maintain security, reliability, and app performance;
  • investigate crashes, errors, abuse, or technical problems.

3. Photo and Face Processing

SnapLoop requires your permission before accessing your photo library or camera. Photo scanning for an Event is limited to photos falling within that Event's eligible date range.

Face Setup is used only to help identify photos of participating users. SnapLoop does not use facial information for advertising or to determine sensitive characteristics such as race, religion, health, or political views.

4. Sharing of Information

SnapLoop does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with other authorized participants of an Event only as needed to provide the Event's photo-sharing functionality.

SnapLoop also uses service providers, including Google Firebase, for functions such as authentication, database storage, cloud storage, notifications, and backend services. These providers may process information on our behalf in accordance with their own terms and privacy protections.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and Event information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SnapLoop, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve technical issues.

Temporary or shared photo data may be removed after the applicable Event or retention period. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period before being deleted through normal backup rotation.

6. Your Choices

You may:

  • deny or revoke Camera or Photos permissions through iOS Settings;
  • disable applicable sharing features within SnapLoop;
  • leave Events where supported;
  • request access to or deletion of your account and associated personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

SnapLoop is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to consent to the processing of their personal information. Parents or guardians should supervise use where required by applicable law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International Processing

SnapLoop uses cloud service providers whose systems may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Appropriate safeguards are used where required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as SnapLoop evolves. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate the latest version.
