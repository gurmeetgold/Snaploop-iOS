SnapLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

SnapLoop helps people participating in shared Events find photos of themselves taken on their own or other participating users' devices. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information SnapLoop processes, why it is processed, how it is protected and shared, how long it is retained, and the choices and rights available to users.

Plain-language summary: SnapLoop does not upload your entire photo library. Face Match is optional. Your Face Setup selfie stays on your iPhone. With your express consent, SnapLoop stores numerical face templates in Firebase and may temporarily provide those templates to authenticated Event-member devices for Event-scoped, on-device matching. Unmatched candidate-face embeddings are not stored as account profiles. SnapLoop does not sell or use biometric information for advertising. Active Face Match consent and account-level numerical face templates expire after 12 months (365 days) without Face Match activity initiated by your account and can be deleted sooner at your request.

1. Information We Process

  • Account information: your verified phone number, account identifier, optional display name, and account settings.
  • Event information: Event name, dates, category, membership, invitations, participant settings, organizer actions, and related Event metadata.
  • Photos and photo-library information: SnapLoop accesses photos only after you grant iOS Photos permission. Event scanning is limited to photos eligible for the Event's selected date range. SnapLoop does not upload your entire photo library.
  • Matched photo previews: optimized JPEG previews of photos identified as containing an eligible Event participant may be uploaded so the matched participant can view the photo in SnapLoop.
  • Face Setup selfie/reference image: when you choose Face Match, a reference image is stored locally on your iPhone. SnapLoop's current architecture does not upload the raw Face Setup selfie/reference image to Firebase.
  • Biometric face-template information: with your express consent, SnapLoop converts Face Setup images into numerical face embeddings/templates used to recognize you in eligible Event photos. SnapLoop treats these templates as sensitive biometric information.
  • Transient candidate-face processing: when an Event participant runs photo matching, SnapLoop may detect faces and temporarily create numerical face representations from faces appearing in eligible Event-date photos so those faces can be compared with consenting participants' templates.
  • Consent and compliance information: SnapLoop records the Face Match policy version, immutable disclosure identifier and disclosure hash, consent, expiry and withdrawal timestamps, app version, platform, locale, consent method, and the country and, where applicable, province/state code you declare for Face Match availability. SnapLoop does not require GPS or a precise address for this jurisdiction check.
  • Technical information: device, diagnostic, crash, security, notification, and service information may be processed as needed by SnapLoop, Apple, Google Firebase, and other infrastructure used to operate the service.

2. How We Use Information

We process personal information only for purposes reasonably connected to operating and protecting SnapLoop, including to:

  • authenticate users and maintain accounts;
  • create, join, administer, and secure Events;
  • perform Face Match for users who have provided active, current consent;
  • identify and deliver matched photos to authorized Event participants;
  • support invitations, notifications, saving, sharing, and Event functionality;
  • determine whether Face Match is available in the jurisdiction a user declares;
  • maintain security, reliability, abuse prevention, and app performance; and
  • investigate crashes, errors, fraud, misuse, or technical problems.

SnapLoop does not use biometric face-template information for advertising, behavioral advertising, account authentication, surveillance, stranger identification, law-enforcement identification, credit or insurance decisions, employment decisions, or to infer sensitive traits such as race, ethnicity, religion, health status, disability, sexual orientation, political views, or similar characteristics.

SnapLoop does not sell, lease, trade, license for advertising, or otherwise profit from biometric face-template information.

3. Face Match Biometric Notice and Express Consent

Face Match is a separate optional feature. Before a current account-level face template can be stored, SnapLoop presents a separate Face Match notice and requires an affirmative I Agree & Continue action. A user's ability to use non-biometric portions of SnapLoop does not depend on accepting Face Match.

What Face Match does

SnapLoop uses a Face Setup selfie to create numerical face embeddings/templates that can recognize you in photos from Events you choose to join. The raw Face Setup selfie/reference image stays on your iPhone. The numerical face template is stored with your SnapLoop account in Firebase.

Server-enforced consent

Face-profile creation and updates pass through SnapLoop's trusted backend. The backend checks that the signed-in user has an active current-version Face Match consent record, an eligible declared jurisdiction, and a valid face-profile payload before storing a numerical template. Direct client writes to cloud face-profile records are denied by database security rules.

When an Event member's device requests templates for matching, the backend re-checks the target participant's current consent, consent-expiry deadline, profile version, and Face Match eligibility before returning that participant's template. A missing, withdrawn, expired, outdated, or ineligible consent record prevents the template from being returned for matching.

Versioned consent evidence

For auditability, SnapLoop stores a versioned disclosure identifier and SHA-256 disclosure hash together with the consent-policy version, server timestamp, app version, platform, locale, consent method, declared jurisdiction code, and server-issued expiry. This evidence is not itself used to recognize a face.

Templates used by Event members' devices

While you participate in an active Event, your numerical template may be provided through SnapLoop's backend only to authenticated participating Event members' devices so that those devices can compare eligible Event-date photos on-device. Templates are not displayed as ordinary user content and are not authorized for use outside the Event-scoped Face Match purpose.

Candidate and bystander faces

Important: To determine whether a group photo contains a consenting participant, a participating iPhone may temporarily detect other faces in that photo and create candidate face embeddings in memory for comparison. SnapLoop's matching workflow does not upload unmatched candidate embeddings, does not save them as account-level face profiles, does not intentionally write them to persistent storage, does not send them to analytics, and does not use them to train the face model. Candidate embeddings exist only as needed to perform the on-device comparison and leave scope before cloud persistence of an accepted match.

Age

Face Match is intended only for users age 18 or older. By selecting I Agree & Continue, a user confirms that they are at least 18 years old and capable of providing the required consent.

4. Jurisdiction and Face Match Availability

Before giving Face Match consent, users select the country where they ordinarily reside. Canada and the United States also require a province/state or territory selection; India does not. SnapLoop stores only the declared jurisdiction code needed for Face Match compliance and does not require GPS or a precise residential address for this purpose.

Face Match is currently offered in India and selected jurisdictions in Canada and the United States. To reduce launch risk in jurisdictions with heightened biometric, sensitive-data, or local facial-recognition requirements, SnapLoop intentionally blocks Face Match in Quebec and in the following U.S. states at launch: Alabama, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Oklahoma, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Virginia, Vermont, and Washington. The rest of the SnapLoop app may remain available where offered.

Jurisdiction availability is a product compliance control, not a statement that a jurisdiction has no privacy law. SnapLoop may restrict or disable Face Match in additional countries, provinces, states, or territories where legal, regulatory, age, registration, consent, or operational requirements have not yet been completed. App availability in an App Store region does not guarantee availability of every SnapLoop feature.

SnapLoop also maintains a private server-side Face Match control that can disable biometric processing globally, block a country, or block additional jurisdiction codes without waiting for an App Store update. This control is intended for legal, regulatory, security, or emergency operational use and cannot override built-in launch restrictions to make a blocked jurisdiction available.

5. Refusing, Withdrawing, Deleting, or Expiring Face Match

You may choose not to activate Face Match. You can review or withdraw Face Match consent at any time from Privacy & Data in SnapLoop. Withdrawal does not require deletion of your entire SnapLoop account.

When withdrawal is successfully completed, SnapLoop stops future Face Match processing for your account, deletes your active account-level face-template information, removes your Face Setup from active matching state, removes related matching metadata from active Event data, and deletes the local Face Setup reference image from that app installation.

You may also delete Face Setup without deleting your account. Deleting Face Setup removes the active face profile and matching derivatives. A separate historical consent record may remain where needed for legal or audit purposes.

If Face Match consent or the account-level template expires due to inactivity, a new Face Setup and new active consent are required before automatic Face Match can resume.

6. Retention and Destruction Schedule

SnapLoop limits retention according to the purpose for which information was collected and deletes information when that purpose has ended or when deletion is requested or required, subject to limited legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, and backup obligations.

  • Active Face Match consent and account-level numerical face templates: expire after 12 months (365 days) without Face Match activity initiated by your account. The initial period begins when you consent. Completing or updating Face Setup and authenticated Face Match activity initiated by your account can restart the 12-month period. Another participant's device merely retrieving or using your template does not extend your retention period. When the server-side period expires, the active face template is deleted and the consent record is marked expired. A new Face Setup and consent are required to resume Face Match.
  • Consent without completed Face Setup: consent has its own server-issued 12-month expiry even if you never complete Face Setup, so an unused consent record cannot remain active indefinitely.
  • Earlier biometric deletion: the active face template is deleted earlier when you delete Face Setup, withdraw Face Match consent, or delete your account.
  • Local Face Setup image: retained only on the device while Face Setup remains locally configured. It is removed by the app when you delete Face Setup, withdraw consent, or delete the account from that installation. Uninstalling the app also removes app-local data according to iOS behavior.
  • Transient candidate-face embeddings: used in memory for the on-device matching operation and are not retained as persistent account-level profiles for people merely appearing in Event photos.
  • Event-related cloud data: Event cloud data, including matched photo previews, is scheduled for deletion within 15 days after an Event ends. If an Event is manually deleted, its Event-related cloud data is scheduled for deletion within 15 days after deletion.
  • Consent-history records: a limited record of consent, withdrawal, expiration, policy version, disclosure evidence, and related audit metadata may be retained for legal, security, dispute, and audit purposes. Such records are not used for face recognition.
  • Account deletion: the in-app Delete Account control initiates deletion of the SnapLoop account and associated account data. Limited records may be retained only where reasonably necessary or legally required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, compliance, or backup rotation.

Where applicable law requires a shorter retention period or earlier destruction than the periods described above, SnapLoop will follow the legally required shorter period.

7. Event Participants, Photo Sharing, and Access Controls

SnapLoop is built around user-created Events. Event membership does not give every member unrestricted access to every photo. Matched photo previews are designed to be readable only by authorized users identified as appearing in the relevant photo, subject to Event membership and sharing controls.

Cloud face profiles are private account data. Database rules allow a user to read only their own cloud face profile directly. Event matching templates are provided through trusted backend logic that re-checks membership, active Event status, consent, consent expiry, profile version, jurisdiction eligibility, and any emergency feature restriction.

8. Service Providers and International Processing

SnapLoop uses service providers to operate the app. Current core providers include:

  • Google Firebase: authentication, database, cloud storage, Cloud Functions/backend operations, notifications, and related infrastructure.
  • Apple: iOS platform functionality, permissions, device security, App Store distribution, and related Apple services.

The face-embedding model used by SnapLoop runs on-device. SnapLoop does not send a user's Face Setup image to the open-source face-model publisher for recognition processing.

Service providers may process information in jurisdictions other than where you live. SnapLoop limits provider use to providing, securing, maintaining, or supporting the service, subject to applicable contractual, technical, organizational, and legal protections.

SnapLoop does not disclose biometric face-template information to advertisers or data brokers.

9. Security

SnapLoop uses safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of biometric and other personal information. Current protections include account authentication, trusted server-side biometric write controls, current-consent and expiry checks before Event template delivery, restricted Firestore and Storage rules, Event-membership checks, a private server-side biometric kill switch/blocklist, local iOS complete file protection for Face Setup reference images, exclusion of local Face Setup reference images from device backups, server-side retention controls, and user-initiated deletion workflows.

SnapLoop seeks to minimize biometric information by storing numerical templates instead of raw Face Setup selfies in the cloud and by keeping candidate-face comparison on-device. No system can guarantee absolute security. If SnapLoop becomes aware of a security incident involving personal information, it will investigate and provide legally required notifications where applicable.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain information about, or restrict certain processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

SnapLoop provides controls that allow you to:

  • deny, limit, or revoke Camera and Photos permissions through iOS Settings;
  • choose not to activate Face Match;
  • review or withdraw Face Match consent;
  • delete Face Setup without deleting the entire account;
  • leave Events where supported;
  • delete your SnapLoop account in the app; and
  • contact us regarding access, correction, deletion, consent, or other privacy requests.

Face Match is intended only for users age 18 or older. SnapLoop does not knowingly intend to create account-level biometric Face Setups for children. If you believe a minor has activated Face Match improperly, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate restriction or deletion steps.

12. No Sale, Advertising Use, or Secondary Biometric Use

SnapLoop does not sell, lease, trade, or monetize biometric face-template information. SnapLoop does not use biometric templates for targeted advertising, cross-app tracking, marketing profiles, credit or insurance decisions, employment decisions, surveillance, law-enforcement identification, or unrelated machine-learning training.

If SnapLoop ever proposes a materially different biometric purpose, it will not apply that new purpose to existing biometric information without first providing an updated disclosure and obtaining new consent where required.

13. Open-Source Face Technology

SnapLoop currently uses an on-device Core ML conversion of AuraFace-v1 to create numerical face embeddings. The AuraFace component used by SnapLoop is distributed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, and the applicable license notice is included with the application. Open-source licensing does not change SnapLoop's privacy commitments: user Face Setup images are not sent to the model publisher for recognition processing.

Open-source components may be updated over time. If a model change materially changes the biometric purpose, type of information processed, or disclosure required by law, SnapLoop will update the applicable notice and obtain renewed consent where required.
