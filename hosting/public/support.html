Quick help

Joining an Event

Open the invitation link or enter the Event invitation information in SnapLoop. You must be an Event member to participate in that Event.

Photo Access

SnapLoop can work with Full Photos Access or Selected Photos access. With Selected Photos, only photos you choose are available for scanning.

Face Match

Face Match is optional. It requires separate express consent and a Face Setup using the signed-in account holder's own face.

My Photos

Matched photos from eligible Event-date photos appear in My Photos when the matching and sharing requirements for that Event are satisfied.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't SnapLoop see all of my Event photos?

If iOS Photos access is set to Selected Photos, SnapLoop can only scan the photos you have allowed.

  1. Open SnapLoop and go to You.
  2. Under Photo Access, choose Add More Photos if you want to add specific photos.
  3. To change the permission to all photos, use Manage Photo Access or open the SnapLoop settings in iOS and choose Full Photos Access.

Event scanning is also limited to photos eligible for the Event's selected date range.

How do I scan for new Event photos?

Open the Event and select Scan Photos, then start the scan. Keep SnapLoop open while a scan is running. If your photo permission is limited, you can select more photos or allow full access before scanning.

Why am I not seeing a photo of me?

Check that you joined the correct Event, completed Face Setup if you are using Face Match, and that the photo falls within the Event's selected date range. The person whose iPhone contains the photo must also have the relevant photo available to SnapLoop and photo sharing enabled for the Event.

If the photo still does not appear, refresh My Photos or run Scan Photos again from the Event.

What does Face Setup store?

Your Face Setup selfie/reference image stays on your iPhone. With your express Face Match consent, SnapLoop stores numerical face-template information used for Event-scoped matching. Face Match is optional and is not required to use non-biometric portions of SnapLoop.

For the complete current disclosure, including retention, candidate-face processing, eligible jurisdictions, and withdrawal controls, read the Face Match Biometric Notice.

Where is Face Match available?

For the current launch, Face Match is offered in India and supported Canadian provinces and territories. It is not currently offered in Quebec or the United States. Availability may be restricted further for legal, security, or operational reasons.

App availability in an App Store region does not guarantee that Face Match is available in that region.

How do I withdraw Face Match consent?

In SnapLoop, open You → Privacy & Data → Face Match Consent. You can review the current notice or withdraw active consent there. Withdrawal does not require deleting your whole SnapLoop account.

How do I delete my SnapLoop account?

SnapLoop provides account deletion inside the app:

  1. Open You.
  2. Open Privacy & Data.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete SnapLoop Account and confirm.

This permanently removes the account and initiates deletion of associated account data, including face data, Event memberships, and photo previews sourced from that account, subject to limited records that may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required as described in the Privacy Policy.

How long is Event data kept?

Event-related cloud data, including matched photo previews, is scheduled for deletion within 15 days after an Event ends or is manually deleted. Face Match consent and account-level numerical face templates have separate retention rules described in the Privacy Policy.

I changed phones or reinstalled SnapLoop. What should I check?

Sign in with your SnapLoop account, review your iOS Photos permission, and confirm your current Face Setup status. The Face Setup reference image is stored locally on the iPhone, so you may need to complete Face Setup again on a new installation even when account-level settings still exist.
